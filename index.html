Aura Farming Simulator

Live on Roblox

Project Overview

An incremental simulator inspired by internet meme culture. Players farm Aura, move through different farming modes, collect auras with their own visual effects and compete on shared leaderboards.

Objective

Turn a simple loop of clicking, farming and upgrading into a long-term progression that players want to return to, with humor that fits the Roblox community.

Development

Built in modular stages: farming and progression systems, an aura collection with an aura roulette, multiplayer interactions, achievements and a world designed for PC, mobile and tablet. Server-side validation keeps progress and purchases fair.

Technology

Roblox Studio, Luau, DataStore saving and server-authoritative gameplay logic.

Our Contribution

Creative direction, game concept and feature ideas, production with modern development tools, and community promotion.

Don't Trust Anyone

In development

Project Overview

A cooperative psychological horror game for one to five players. Chapter 1 takes place in Blackwood, where a hospital and a forest hide the truth behind Project Mirror, and where someone on your team may not be who they seem.

Objective

Build tension through mystery, exploration and trust. Players investigate together, make group decisions and learn that the greatest threat can look like a friend.

Development

Chapter 1 content is complete: Blackwood Hospital, Blackwood Forest, storytelling through photographs and records found in the world, branching choices, and a cinematic atmosphere shaped by sound design. Testing is the next step.

Technology

Roblox Studio, Luau, multiplayer systems and group decisions recorded by the server.

Our Contribution

Creative direction, story and game concept, production with modern development tools, and community promotion.
